# Data Model: Replay Screening Refinement

- **LegalInstrumentRecord**: `instrument_type`, retrievable `reference`, `governs_source_built_artifact`, and publication/redistribution restrictions.
- **ReplayReadinessEvidence**: one accepted `hermeticity_class` and an `evidence_reference`.
- **CandidateRecord metadata**: recorded governing instrument, hermeticity class, and `historical_build_rate_qualification=deferred_to_replay_environment` on early eligibility.
