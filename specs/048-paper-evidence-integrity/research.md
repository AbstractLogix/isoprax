# Research Decisions: Research Paper Evidence Integrity

## Decisions

### Scope

Keep paper A, the cross-family outcome-commensurability contract, as the main manuscript. Treat model-role specialization and pooling as a companion empirical study. Its synthetic question families do not establish JIT/AIOps outcome equivalence.

### Claim framing

Do not claim that calibration's relation to its declared outcome is a new statistical discovery. The candidate contribution is the explicit outcome-definition contract, its operation boundary, and executable checks that expose when a shared numeric scale does not support a shared interpretation. Novelty remains subject to external review.

### Raw evidence

Use frozen raw rows and exact replay archives. Package the four Bouleusis JSONL files from commit `9f239d6f59cd22e1c1ebf1b9d86b0f28a8fc45b5` losslessly because the supporting repository is private. Do not alter source records. Preserve original SHA-256 values and report source-reconstruction limits.

### Analysis limits

Exact duplicate runs establish repeatability only for the frozen cases and runtime. They do not establish independent replication. Zero observed joint errors do not establish independence or zero population error. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on the declared synthetic sampling unit.

### Validation status

Automated integrity checks establish computational reproducibility for registered artifacts. They do not provide independent scientific review, external validity, real-world outcome equivalence, or Semadmit policy evidence.
