## Positioning

The calibration/target relationship is not a novel statistical discovery. Forecast pooling is not new. The candidate IsoPrax contribution is narrower: provide a forecast-specific structural check for direct pooling and publish a synthetic challenge for proposed operation-specific rules. The public checker and the separate challenge rule have different scopes. Novelty and practical value require independent review.

## Focused independent related-work check

This is an agent-led source review, independent of the earlier citation selection. It is not an external scientific review or a systematic search. We compared the named works with the implemented IsoPrax checker and synthetic challenge.

| Work and checked version | Established capability | Implication for IsoPrax |
|---|---|---|
| Freiesleben and Zezulka (2026), [published article](https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2026.10280) | Treats benchmark scores as measurements. | Bitol [ODCS v3.2.0](https://bitol-io.github.io/open-data-contract-standard/v3.2.0/) and [Data Contract CLI](https://docs.datacontract.com/open-data-contract-standard) | Records dataset semantics, quality, shared definitions, and custom properties. The CLI can [resolve definitions](https://docs.datacontract.com/semantics) and [execute data checks](https://docs.datacontract.com/testing), including custom checks. | A general contract plus a custom policy can express the fields and refusal rule that IsoPrax uses. Native ODCS checks do not by themselves prove outcome equivalence. We did not implement this comparator or measure effort and reliability. |

**Answer to the substitution test.** No current result shows a capability that IsoPrax supplies and that established validity practice plus ODCS and a policy engine cannot supply with comparable effort and reliability. The narrow implemented difference is a forecast-specific interface that compares declared event, observation process, window, and threshold fields for direct pooling. A separate synthetic rule covers more operations. The difference is packaging and focus until an independently specified comparator shows a reliability or effort advantage. We therefore withdraw any implication of unique capability or established incremental value.

**Minimum next test.** Give independent reviewers the same cases and target definitions. Implement one IsoPrax path and one ODCS plus policy path. Predeclare permitted operations, valid semantic bridges, false permissions, unnecessary refusals, implementation effort, and maintenance changes. Include cases authored and labeled outside the IsoPrax team. This comparison remains prospective; the present paper does not report its result.
