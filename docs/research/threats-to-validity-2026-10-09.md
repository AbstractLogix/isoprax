# Threats to validity — 2026-10-09

## Construct validity

The synthetic forecast lanes define their outcomes in advance. They do not show that any real JIT defect label and operational failure label measure a shared event. The operation checker compares recorded declarations. It cannot verify that data collection, censoring, adjudication, or deployment practice matches those declarations.

The 23-case policy challenge uses evaluator labels authored in the same project as the candidate. The model-role data use authored synthetic questions and relevance labels. These records do not establish validity for natural question families or operational targets.

## Internal validity

Benchmark A is deterministic and uses fixed forecasts. It is a constructed counterexample, not a stochastic study. The policy comparison can reflect case construction choices. The frozen model-role runs share the same prompts, cases, and runtime identity; the byte-identical runs are one repeated execution, not independent trials.

## Statistical conclusion validity

The forecast benchmark is not a sample from a defined population. The policy challenge has 23 cases. Reported counts are exact for those cases but are not population rates. The 10 order seeds in the one-bug retrieval experiment are perturbations of one bug, not 10 independent tasks. Model-role bootstrap intervals resample fixed cases or query clusters; they do not correct authored-case selection or support population generalization.

Four forecast calibration-revision cases (`FP02`, `FP03`, `FP05`, and `FP06`) remain unresolved. They are excluded from positive policy-validity claims until valid reference evidence exists. Their outcomes are not repaired by changing the evaluation label after scoring.

## External validity

No production JIT or AIOps dataset pair was evaluated. No operational observation process or cross-domain semantic bridge was validated. These results do not establish field prediction performance, real-world commensurability, or deployment value.

## Conclusion validity and claim scope

The result refutes one sufficiency argument: equal calibration summaries alone do not establish a common outcome. It does not show that pooling is always invalid. Explicit mixture estimands and common-utility decisions can be valid under their own declarations. The paper reports no population estimate of false permission or unnecessary refusal.

## Reproducibility and rights

The local integrity suite checks registered inputs, hashes, computations, and rendered tables. A pass establishes mechanical reproduction of those exact files, not independent scientific validation. The owner authorized four exact Bouleusis raw records under CC BY 4.0. The separate rights audit records that the model-role outputs have no asserted reuse license and are not covered by that grant. Until this is resolved, the full package is not ready for permanent public deposit.

## Required evidence to reduce these threats

1. A blinded target-pair set authored and adjudicated independently of the rule authors, with positive semantic bridges and negative observation-process counterexamples.
2. At least one real dataset pair with independently reviewed event mapping, data-collection process, horizon, population, and label adjudication.
3. Prospective comparison against the predeclared naive, global-label, and metadata-only baselines. Treat target pair or independently authored case as the unit; report false permissions, refusals, interpretation errors, and decisions only when specified.
4. A licensed public artifact deposit with a persistent identifier and a clean-checkout reproduction record.
