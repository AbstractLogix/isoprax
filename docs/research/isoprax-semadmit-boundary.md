# IsoPrax and Semadmit Boundary

## IsoPrax owns public research

IsoPrax defines and evaluates public concepts for measurement validity, outcome semantics, calibration, provenance, evidence admissibility, and permitted comparison. It publishes reference rules, synthetic counterexamples, public-data methods, and claim limits.

IsoPrax may keep public verifiers and reproducible admission examples. These show how a declared research rule can be checked. They do not make IsoPrax a private trust product.

## Semadmit owns product enforcement

Semadmit may operationalize mature IsoPrax findings in its private trust, admission, and verification engine. Its production service owns caller identity, product policy, deployment, enforcement, private data handling, and operational support. These are boundary examples, not a description of uninspected private internals.

## Handoff rule

1. IsoPrax publishes an implementation-neutral rule, its assumptions, and evidence for and against it.
2. Semadmit chooses whether the rule is mature enough for a product use case.
3. Semadmit implements and validates its own production behavior against its needs.
4. Product success does not become evidence for an IsoPrax research claim unless the data and method are publishable and the claim is independently evaluated.

Share public schemas or conceptual contracts only when they aid reproducibility. Do not import private code, policies, algorithms, or architecture into this repository.
