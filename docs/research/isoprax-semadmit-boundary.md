# IsoPrax and Semadmit Boundary

## IsoPrax owns public research

IsoPrax defines and evaluates public concepts for measurement validity, outcome semantics, calibration, provenance, evidence admissibility, and permitted comparison. It publishes reference rules, synthetic counterexamples, public-data methods, and claim limits.

IsoPrax may keep public verifiers and reproducible admission examples. These show how a declared research rule can be checked. They do not make IsoPrax a runtime enforcement system.

## Semadmit owns runtime admission and verification enforcement

Semadmit owns runtime admission and verification enforcement. It decides how to operationalize mature IsoPrax findings in its runtime policies and checks. This boundary states responsibility and does not define Semadmit's implementation.

## Handoff rule

1. IsoPrax publishes an implementation-neutral rule, its assumptions, and evidence for and against it.
2. Semadmit decides whether the rule is mature enough for one of its use cases.
3. Semadmit implements and validates its runtime behavior against its requirements.
4. Operational outcomes do not become evidence for an IsoPrax research claim unless the data and method are publishable and the claim is independently evaluated.

Share public schemas or conceptual contracts only when they aid reproducibility. IsoPrax does not define Semadmit's implementation, policies, algorithms, or architecture.
